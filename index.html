00 / BEGINNING

Walking 
the city

For three years, we documented the streets of Mohyeon, capturing its corners and fleeting moments to understand the source of the unease we had experienced while navigating the neighborhood. 


After six rounds of exploration, what had once felt like a vague, almost intangible sense of anxiety began to emerge as a concrete and clearly identifiable “phenomenon.”

02 / QUESTION

Finding
Solutions

We realized that the reason a familiar street can feel frightening at night is not simply a matter of lighting. Rather, it is shaped by a combination of factors, including the enclosure of the alleys, illumination levels, and pedestrians’ visibility.

We therefore examined how these interconnected factors exist around Mohyun Elementary School and how they interact with one another, seeking possible directions for addressing the problem.
